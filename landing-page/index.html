The quiet details

Small things that
add up to peace of mind

Beyond reminders, Sahay+ quietly weaves the everyday moments of care into one calm place.

Wellness check-ins

Every day, one honest answer: great, okay, or not great. Over time it paints a gentle picture of how Dad is really doing — shared with the family, never graded.

Messages, not checklists

A short note between check-ins — “Did you eat?”, “Call me when free” — sent and read in the same calm place as everything else.

Refill countdowns

When a strip is running low, Sahay+ whispers before it's urgent — “Refill in 5 days” — so no one discovers an empty blister on Sunday night.

Always in sync

Mark a dose on one phone and everyone sees it — instantly, on every device, without refreshing. Care stays current even when families don't.

Getting started

Ready in five minutes

No apps to install. No accounts to create. Open the link, choose your role, start your first day of care.

1
Choose your role

Open Sahay+ on any device. Tap "I receive care" or "I provide care." Your dashboard is ready immediately — no form to fill out.

2
Add your medications

Name, dose, and time. Sahay+ fills in plain-language explanations automatically. Add a personal note or a wellness goal if you'd like.

3
Share with your family

Sahay+ creates a simple care code — like “Maple-42” — share it with the family member who cares for you, and they'll see a live, calm view of your day. Care starts in seconds.
