Everything that matters

Small things that make every day easier

Gentle reminders

A soothing three-note chime at each dose time, with a big card showing what to take and how.

Voice confirmation

Say "Dawai le li" or "I took it". Sahay+ understands Hindi, English and a mix of both.

One-tap SOS

A red SOS button on every screen alerts the family instantly with a siren on their phone.

Morning check-in

"How are you feeling?" One tap tells the family how their day is starting.

Calm night mode

After 9 PM the screen turns dark and gentle, so late-night checks are easy on the eyes.

Doctor-ready summary

Print a clean adherence report before appointments, instead of answering "mostly".

Why we built it

Built with our own grandparents

We watched our families juggle foil strips, missed doses and anxious phone calls. So we tested every screen in their living rooms, with big buttons, no passwords to remember, and words they actually use.
