A day with Sahay+

From the first chime to a quiet goodnight

What an ordinary day looks like, for Dada and for you.

  1. 7:30 AM

    Morning medicines

    A soft chime plays and a big card shows the pill's photo, colour and "before breakfast". He taps I took it, or just says "dawai le li".

  2. Live

    You see it instantly

    Your dashboard ticks the dose off the moment it's taken. No call needed.

  3. 9:30 AM

    "How are you feeling?"

    One tap: Feeling Great, Doing Okay or Not Feeling Great. If he hasn't checked in by 9:30, the app gently nudges him, and you're told at 9:45.

  4. +15 min

    A dose runs late

    If a medicine isn't confirmed 15 minutes after its time, the chime gets louder and you get an overdue alert, so you can send a gentle reminder.

  5. 9:00 PM

    Night mode

    The screen turns dark and calm for the evening dose, easy on tired eyes.

  6. 10:30 PM

    Close the day

    You close the day in one tap: "6 of 6 taken". The streak grows, and tomorrow starts fresh.

When something's wrong

Help is one tap away. A false alarm is one tap to cancel.

SOS with a 10-second safety net

Pressing SOS starts a large countdown. Pressed by mistake? One big "Cancel" button. Otherwise the family is alerted right away.

Location follows

If the phone allows it, a map link with their location is sent to the caregiver straight after the alert. The alert itself never waits for GPS.

Call family, doctor or 112

"Call help" lists the family's emergency contacts and the doctor, with one-tap calling and 112 always at the bottom.

"Are you okay?" safety check

A hard shake or jolt of the phone asks "Are you okay?". If there's no answer in 5 minutes, the caregiver is told.

Sahay+ helps families respond faster. It is not a replacement for emergency services. In an emergency, always call 112.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Does my parent need to know how to use a smartphone?

Barely. The home screen shows one medicine and one big "I took it" button. You set everything up from your phone: medicines, times, photos and contacts.

What happens if they forget to tap the button?

The reminder stays on screen. Fifteen minutes after the dose time the chime gets louder and you get an overdue alert, so you can message or call them.

Can more than one family member help?

Yes. A second caregiver can link with the same 6-letter care code and sees everything you see. You can also hand over care for a few days when you're travelling.

Which languages does voice confirmation understand?

Hindi, English and a mix of both. Phrases like "dawai le li" or "I took my Dolo" work.

What if SOS is pressed by accident?

There's a 10-second countdown with a large "Cancel (False Alarm)" button before anything is sent. After an alert, "I'm okay" lets them tell the family they're safe.

Does it work on tablets and computers?

Yes. It runs in any modern browser, the caregiver dashboard works on laptops and phones, and there's an Android app too.

Is Sahay+ a medical device or a replacement for a doctor?

No. Sahay+ helps families stay on top of medicines a doctor has already prescribed. It doesn't give medical advice. Always follow your doctor's instructions, and call 112 in an emergency.
